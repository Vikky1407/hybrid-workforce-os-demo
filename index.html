What format does my file need?
  • Event log (required): one row per step, with Case ID, Activity, User, Start and End time, and any case attributes such as type or amount. A Work Genome process works as it is.
  • Step facts (optional): for each step, whether it is a control, whether a wrong action can be undone, and its data (Structured, Mixed or Text). Without it, these are inferred from step and application names.
  • Shadow review (optional): the agent's decision on real cases next to the decisions of a panel of people. This is Gate 1 of the autonomy ladder.
  • Live weeks (optional): weekly results after go-live: drafts approved, sampled errors, case-mix shift, model changes.

    Connect your own AI

    The tool works fully without AI. Connecting your own model lets you test a real AI agent the same way the demo agents are tested: on real cases, against a panel of people.

    1. Get an API key from your provider: Anthropic or OpenAI. At work, use a key your IT team has approved.
    2. Paste it below and press Connect. It stays in this browser tab and is gone when you close it.
    3. Go to step 4, Autonomy ladder, and press "Shadow-test your AI on 60 cases" on any agent step. Its score appears next to the panel's, with a pass or fail on Gate 1.

    Only the text of those 60 cases is sent, straight from your browser to the provider. A test costs a few cents.
