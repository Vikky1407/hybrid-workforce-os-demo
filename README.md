# Hybrid Workforce OS

**The Autonomous Enterprise · tool 03 of 05**

Every AI agent business case asks "how much can we automate?". Hybrid Workforce OS asks what the work should look like, and who or what should do each part. For any process it removes the waste first, decides step by step what goes to people, rules automation or AI agents, replays every case through the new design, lets each agent earn its autonomy against a panel of people, and separates hours released from money saved.

**Try it:** https://vikky1407.github.io/hybrid-workforce-os-demo/
